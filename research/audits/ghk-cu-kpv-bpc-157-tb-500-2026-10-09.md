### Content boundary

The query asks for a dosage and frequency chart. The boundary set 2026-09-24 holds: no dose figure
and no frequency figure for this combination is reproduced anywhere in the refresh, because none
exists in published research to reproduce.

One number is now on the page that was not there before: the 80 mg vial total, with the component
ratio named in words rather than figures. This is reported as a manufacturing specification that
identifies the product, and the sentence that follows it states that no study administered that
ratio to a person or an animal. The judgement is that product identity is reportable and dose
quantity is not. The reconstitution section deliberately describes the shape of the arithmetic
without supplying a worked example, so the page explains why the charts are hollow without
reprinting one.
